The Spokane River

A page about this beautiful river

The Spokane River is a great place to watch stunning sunrises. From the water especially, its a senic sight in the mornings.

The Spokane River is about 111 miles long. It starts at Lake Coeur D'Alene in Idaho and empties into the Columbia River.

Fun facts about the river

  • The river has seven hydroelectric dams along it.
  • In the past, the river was often used for salmon fishing by local tribes.
  • Both the Gonzaga Women's and Men's Rowing teams use the river for their sport.
